01 / About me

About me

Hi, I'm Sean Ashley Reyes. My favorite projects are the ones where the system works well and also looks and feels good to use.

I'm a fourth-year BS Information Technology student at La Consolacion University Philippines in Malolos, Bulacan. I build mobile apps and web systems, usually taking the UI/UX and frontend side of a project and connecting it to a real backend.

Alongside development I do multimedia work: video editing, narration, graphic design and photo editing. I lean heavily on AI throughout: for prototyping, coding, research and documentation, and inside the products themselves. Most of what I build is aimed at real problems in the Philippines.

03 / Projects

Projects

Mobile

SmartEvent

Capstone project · UI/UX lead and frontend developer

A mobile app that student organizations under LCUP CITE use for events, inventory, budgets and reports, with Officer, Faculty Adviser and Admin roles.

  • Designed the visual system, a “docket stub” look inspired by receipts and tickets
  • Built role-based dashboards, live budget charts and a two-stage event approval flow
  • Connected the app to a FastAPI + Supabase backend and got a backend fix merged through a pull request
  • Flutter
  • FastAPI
  • Supabase
